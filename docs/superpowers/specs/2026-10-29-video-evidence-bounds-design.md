A. Read-only whole-chain audit before implementation. Distinguish existing protection from missing protection. Produce a concrete minimal change contract with failure examples and tests, not a speculative full rewrite.
B. Implement reviewed evidence/request-bound contract. Preserve all raw frames/claims/questions. Structural invalid events must reach existing bounded correction before spending further revisit capacity. Model requests must obey60 image cap and all mandatory audit anchors; packet metadata/ownership/source evidence must not be silently dropped to fit. Revisit capacity exhaustion must be explicit and durable, never a fabricated no_new_frames result or confirmed evidence. Avoid an unconditional append-until-failure loop. Remove compiler/provenance transport duplication rather than business facts. Keep input/navigation identities, prior last-event continuity and independent audit.
